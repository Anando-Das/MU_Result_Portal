Getting Here

Metropolitan's campus is centrally located and easily accessible from the city and surrounding regions.

By Car

The main entrance is located at 1 Metropolitan Drive. Visitor parking is available in Lot C near the Visitor Center. A daily permit ($8) is purchased at the kiosk.

By Public Transport

Take the Metro Line 2 to "University Station," then walk 5 minutes north. Numerous bus routes also stop directly on campus.

By Air

The nearest airport is Metropolitan International Airport (MIA), 18 km from campus. Taxi, rideshare, and shuttle services are readily available.

Nearby Lodging

The Metropolitan Guest House (on campus) and several nearby hotels offer preferred visitor rates. Call ahead for availability.
