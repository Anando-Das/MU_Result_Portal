Our History

From a small college founded in 1885 to a world-class research university — the story of Metropolitan is one of relentless ambition.

1885
Founding of Metropolitan University
Established with a mission of discovery and service, Metropolitan opened its doors with 30 students and a single building on the edge of the city.
1912
First Olympic Medalists
Metropolitan athletes competed in the Olympics for the first time, beginning a tradition of Olympic excellence that continues to this day.
1952
Graduate School Expansion
The university launched its first doctoral programs, transforming Metropolitan into a leading research institution.
1985
Centennial and Global Growth
On its 100th anniversary, Metropolitan enrolled students from over 100 countries and established its first international research partnerships.
2010
AI & Innovation Center Opens
The Metropolitan AI Lab launched, quickly becoming one of the top artificial intelligence research centers in the world.
Today
Leading the Next Century
With $94M in annual research funding and students from 150+ countries, Metropolitan continues to shape the future across every field of human endeavor.
