Financial Aid & Scholarships

At Metropolitan, we believe that financial circumstances should never be a barrier to a world-class education. Our commitment to meeting 100% of demonstrated financial need for all admitted undergraduates is one of the most generous aid policies in higher education.

For families with incomes below $150,000, tuition is generally fully covered. Our financial aid advisors are available year-round to help you navigate every step of the process.
